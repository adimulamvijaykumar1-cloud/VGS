Common Questions

What classes are available?
We currently offer coaching for Classes 6th to 10th under our IIT Foundation program, along with dedicated Sainik School entrance batches and Olympiad preparation batches for eligible age groups.
What is IIT Foundation?
IIT Foundation is a structured program that builds strong fundamentals in Mathematics and Science from an early stage, so students are well-prepared for competitive exams like IIT-JEE in the future.
Do you conduct weekly tests?
Yes. Every batch has weekly tests followed by detailed performance analysis, which is also shared with parents during regular parent meetings.
